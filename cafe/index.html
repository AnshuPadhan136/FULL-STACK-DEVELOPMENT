our story

More Than Just
a Coffee Shop

Ember & Bloom started with a simple idea: what if your neighborhood cafe felt like a friend's living room? A place where the barista remembers your name, the pastries are baked before sunrise, and the Wi-Fi password is written on a chalkboard next to a doodle of a cat.

"We don't just make coffee. We make the best part of your morning."

Every bean is sourced from small farms we've visited personally. Every recipe is tested obsessively until it's just right. And every corner of our space is designed to make you want to stay a little longer.

  • ☕ Single-origin, ethically sourced beans
  • 🌿 Seasonal menu that changes with the harvest
  • 🍞 Everything baked in-house, from scratch, daily
  • 💛 A community space, not just a business
